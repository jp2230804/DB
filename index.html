In Google Sheets, go to File → Share → Publish to web, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), press Publish, and paste the link below.

Open my shelf
ColumnWhat to put in it
BGGIDThe number in the BoardGameGeek address, e.g. 128882 for Avalon. Adds a link to the game's BGG page.
NAMEGame name. Required. (Also accepted: 名稱)
PLAYER2-5, 2~5, 4, 2,4,6 or 3+ (人數)
PLAYTIMEMinutes: 30-60, 30~150, 45, or hours like 2h (時間)
GAMETYPETypes separated by 、 or commas: 家庭、策略 (類型)
IMAGEAny picture link from any website: BGG, Imgur, Google Drive, Dropbox, GitHub, a shop page's image... Drive, Dropbox and Imgur page links are converted automatically. (Also accepted: IMG, 圖片)
DESCRIPTIONA note shown under the name (備註)
TAGA short label shown on the game's picture, like 推薦 or New. Separate several with 、 (標籤)
